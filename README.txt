Digonto Player 1.0  -  play video and music
===========================================
Part of the Digonto apps (digonto.dev). Install with the installer in the folder above this one (see ../README.txt).

Plays MP4, MKV, WebM, AVI, MOV, MPEG, MP3, FLAC, WAV, OGG, Opus, M4A, AAC and more (decoding is built into Qt Multimedia,
nothing else is installed). Open files or a whole folder, drop them on the window, or open a web address.

PLAY LIST   side panel (L): double-click to play, drag to re-order, Add / Clear. File > Save play list makes an .m3u file;
            .m3u lists open like any file. Shuffle (H), repeat off / all / one (R).
KEYS        Space play/pause, S stop, N next, B previous, Left/Right 5 s (Shift 30 s, Ctrl 5 min), Up/Down volume, M mute,
            [ and ] speed, \ normal speed, F full screen (Esc leaves), L play list, T always on top, Ctrl+Shift+S picture of this moment.
MOUSE       click = play/pause, double-click = full screen, wheel = volume. In full screen the controls hide while you watch.
FILES       audio tracks and subtitles that are inside the file: Audio > Audio track / Subtitles.
            Long films (over 10 minutes) continue where you stopped.
Limits      subtitle files next to the video (.srt) are not read yet; DVD / Blu-ray discs are not supported.
            Tested here without a screen or speakers; please report bugs after your own testing.
